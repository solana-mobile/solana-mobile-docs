title: "Bug Bounty Program"
description: "Report vulnerabilities in the Solana Mobile ecosystem and earn SKR rewards."
---

Solana Mobile has a bug bounty program for Seed Vault, the SKR onchain programs, and Seeker Genesis Token infrastructure.

- [Read the full Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://solanamobile.com/vulnerability-disclosure-policy-and-bug-bounty-web)

Vulnerability Disclosures must include the **documentation requirements** listed in our policy, including a summary, the affected component, numbered reproduction steps, a working proof-of-concept, and your impact assessment. Keep reports private until coordinated disclosure.

Apply through our [Bug Bounty Program Form](https://security.solanamobile.com).
