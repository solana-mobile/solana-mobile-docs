## 1. Navigate to the Publisher Portal

Log into the [Solana dApp Publisher Portal](https://publish.solanamobile.com) and connect your publisher wallet.

## 2. Update your listing

If needed, you can change your app details (e.g. name, descriptions, preview assets) under **Store Listing** in the sidebar. Save a draft to keep your changes for later, or publish them.

Publishing listing changes creates a new release with the same APK as your current version. You mint a Release NFT and the release goes through review, so your changes reach the dApp Store once it is published. To ship listing changes together with a new APK, keep them as a draft and submit a new version instead.

## 3. Submit an updated release version

Go to **Releases** in the sidebar and press **New Version**.

Before you upload a new APK, ensure:

- The APK is a release build signed with the same Android signing key you used for your initial release.
- The package name is the same as in your initial release.
- The `versionName` and `versionCode` value is properly incremented between each update.

Upload the APK or use an APK URL, and fill out **What's New**. This section is displayed to users on your dApp Store listing page.

Press **Submit** and approve each transaction in your wallet as it is requested.

Congratulations on submitting an app update! 🎉

## After submission

Once submitted, the new release automatically enters the app review queue and will immediately go live once approved.

### App Review

- Review results are sent to your developer email from `publishersupport@dappstore.solanamobile.com` typically within 1-2 business days.
- Apps are reviewed in the order they are submitted.
- If you haven't received a response after 5 business days, submit an **App Review Inquiry** in the `#dev-answers` channel on [Discord](http://discord.gg/solanamobile).

### Get Support

For questions about publishing, app review, or launching on the dApp Store, see the [dApp Store Support](/dapp-store/support) page.

Join the [Solana Mobile Discord](http://discord.gg/solanamobile), get the **Developer** role, and use the `#dev-answers` channel for assistance.
