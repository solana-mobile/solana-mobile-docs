## Before you begin

Ensure you have:

- A release APK signed with your release key. Debug builds are rejected, and the APK must support `arm64-v8a` and be at most 2 GB. See [Build and Sign an APK](/dapp-store/build-and-sign-an-apk).
- Listing assets: a 512×512 icon with a background color (no transparency), a 1200×600 banner, and 4 to 8 screenshots. See the [Listing Page Guidelines](/dapp-store/listing-page-guidelines).
- A public website about your app, your company, or you as the developer.
- A terms of service and a privacy policy, each on its own publicly reachable HTTPS page.
- A Solana wallet browser extension (Solflare, Phantom, etc.) with a small amount of SOL. Use a new wallet that you only use for publishing this app.
- Reviewed the [Publisher Policy](/dapp-store/publisher-policy) and [Developer Agreement](/dapp-store/agreement).

<Tip>
  See [Pocket Address](https://github.com/beeman/pocket-address) for a complete
  example: an app, its listing assets, and a website with a terms of service and
  privacy policy.
</Tip>

## 1. Sign up for a Publisher Account

Navigate to the [Publisher Portal](https://publish.solanamobile.com) and sign up with your email address, name, and password. Enter the code sent to your email to log in.

## 2. Create your publisher profile

Choose whether you are publishing as an individual or as an organization, then enter your name and website.

Your website must be publicly reachable and about your app, your company, or you as the developer.

## 3. Verify your identity

Complete KYC if you publish as an individual, or KYB if you publish as a company. You can skip this when you create your profile, but you can't add a dApp until you are approved.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/static/publishing_portal/verify-identity.png"
    alt="Identity verification screen with KYC and KYB options and an approved status"
  />
</Frame>

If you get stuck, get the **Developer** role in the `#developer` channel on [Discord](https://discord.gg/solanamobile), then open a **KYC / KYB Verification Issue** ticket in `#dev-answers`. Tickets are private to you and the Solana Mobile team.

## 4. Connect your publisher wallet

Press **Connect Wallet** and connect your wallet extension. This will be your _publisher wallet_.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/static/publishing_portal/connect-wallet.png"
    alt="Publisher Portal prompting to connect a wallet before adding a dApp"
  />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Your publisher wallet is required for all future submissions of this app. Do
  not lose access to it or you will not be able to make new submissions of this
  app.
</Warning>

## 5. Add your dApp details

Fill out the form with your app details. The listing preview on the right shows how your app will look in the dApp Store.

- The **Package Name** must exactly match the application ID in your APK. It is case-sensitive, must not be used by another app, and can't be changed later.
- Add your app website, support email, contact email, and the links to your terms of use and privacy policy. Every link must be a public HTTPS page; the portal checks each one.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/static/publishing_portal/dapp-details.png"
    alt="dApp details form with website, emails, terms of use, and privacy policy links next to the listing preview"
  />
</Frame>

Press **Save** and approve the transaction in your wallet. This mints your App NFT. To finish later without minting, press **Save and continue later**.

<Note>
  Uploads use Cloudflare R2 by default. To use ArDrive or AWS S3 instead,
  change the storage provider in **Settings** > **Preferences**.
</Note>

See the [Listing Page Guidelines](/dapp-store/listing-page-guidelines) for detailed requirements.

## 6. Submit your first release version

Go to **Releases** in the sidebar and press **New Version**.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/static/publishing_portal/releases-new-version.png"
    alt="Empty Releases page with the New Version button"
  />
</Frame>

Upload your signed APK or use an APK URL, then describe the release in **What's New**.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/static/publishing_portal/upload-release.png"
    alt="Upload your dApp Release form with an APK file selected and the What's New field"
  />
</Frame>

Press **Submit**. The portal processes your APK and publishes the release onchain. Approve each transaction in your wallet as it is requested.

<Warning>
  Ensure you approve each signing request and do not skip any, or your
  submission will not complete.
</Warning>

Once processed, your release shows as **In Review**.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/static/publishing_portal/release-in-review.png"
    alt="Release details page showing version 1.0.0 in review with its onchain details"
  />
</Frame>

Congratulations on submitting your first app! 🎉

## After Submission

Once submitted, your app automatically enters the app review queue and will go live immediately under an appropriate category once approved.

### Common rejection reasons

<Warning>

- Your publisher website is unreachable or not about your app, your company, or you as the developer.
- Your terms of service or privacy policy links are broken or not publicly reachable.

</Warning>

### App Review

- Review results are sent to your developer email from `publishersupport@dappstore.solanamobile.com` typically within 3-4 business days.
- Apps are reviewed in the order they are submitted.
- If you haven't received a response after 5 business days, submit an **App Review Inquiry** in the `#dev-answers` channel on [Discord](http://discord.gg/solanamobile).

### Get Support

For questions about publishing, app review, or launching on the dApp Store, see the [dApp Store Support](/dapp-store/support) page.

Join the [Solana Mobile Discord](http://discord.gg/solanamobile), get the **Developer** role, and use the `#dev-answers` channel for assistance.
