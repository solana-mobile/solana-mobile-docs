We have two separate initiatives to double down on security in the Solana Mobile ecosystem: the Solana Mobile Vulnerability Disclosure Policy, which includes our Bug Bounty Program, and Solana Mobile Security Grants.

[Read more in our blog](https://solanamobile.com/blog/introducing-the-solana-mobile-vulnerability-disclosure-policy-bug-bounty-program-and-security-grants)

## Bug Bounty Program

Solana Mobile has a bug bounty program for Seed Vault, the SKR onchain programs, and Seeker Genesis Token infrastructure. Valid reports earn up to **$75,000 in SKR**.

- [Read the full Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://solanamobile.com/vulnerability-disclosure-policy-and-bug-bounty-web)
- [Supplemental Vulnerability Disclosure Policy Terms](https://solanamobile.com/vdp-supplemental-terms-web)

Vulnerability Disclosures must include the **documentation requirements** listed in [our policy](https://solanamobile.com/vulnerability-disclosure-policy-and-bug-bounty-web), including a summary, the affected component, numbered reproduction steps, a working proof-of-concept, and your impact assessment. Keep reports private until coordinated disclosure.
